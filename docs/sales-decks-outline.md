## 1. Fund Accounting deck
**Audience:** GPs, CFOs, fund accountants, boutique administrators evaluating the ledger.

1. Problem: spreadsheet/month-end rekeying, fragile NAV, audit scramble  
2. Product: double-entry GL, automated NAV, waterfalls, 12+ reports, AI extract-with-human-post  
3. Who buys: self-administering managers + fund admins/CSPs  
4. Proof: Kumari Capital case study (mutual-fund volume; methodology caveats)  
5. Pricing pointer: manager AUM tiers + admin Studio/Practice/Firm  
6. Implementation: ~2 weeks greenfield; 8–12 weeks enterprise migration  
7. Security: link `/security`, DD pack under NDA  
8. Next step: demo with their chart of accounts / sample statements  

## 2. SPV Fund Management deck
**Audience:** Syndicate leads, operators, emerging managers running deal vehicles.

1. Problem: formation + KYC + carry + reporting across tools  
2. Product: formation → onboarding → capital → carry → ledger → member portal  
3. Pricing: Single SPV USD 3,900 · Syndicate 2% (min 3,500 / max 10,000) · Platform custom  
4. Payment trigger: first LP payment **or 60 days after first invite accepted**  
5. Contrast: free-through-first-close is for first **funds**, not single-deal SPVs  
6. Competitive posture: published pricing; same ledger as funds (see compare pages)  
7. Next step: start a deal / Launch Free if eligible path  

## 3. Fund Administration & LP Portal deck
**Audience:** GPs who want investor experience + back office without a full admin firm.

1. Problem: portal bolted onto accounting; capital calls in email  
2. Product: white-label portal + KYC/AML + calls/distributions/fees/carry  
3. Modular: runs with Fund Accounting or alone; shares data model  
4. Launch Free: free through first close (up to 10 LPs); conversion tracking live  
5. Implementation: ~2 weeks for portal-first onboarding  
6. Next step: claim Launch Free or book demo
